FLG: kind (d directory, l link, - file), L locked, I invisible.
DATA/RESOURCE: logical fork sizes; blank for directories.
TYPE/CREA: Macintosh type/creator; '.' marks nonprintable bytes.
CREATED: HFS creation date/time. NAME: encoded filename.
Sizes are bytes; zero means an empty fork.
FLAGS also has N name-locked, B bundle; HEX is raw Finder flags.
